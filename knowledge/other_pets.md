# Other Pets

General guidance only, not veterinary advice.

## About These Guides

The PawPal+ care guides mainly cover dogs and cats. Rabbits, birds, reptiles, fish, and small mammals like hamsters and guinea pigs have very different needs for feeding and diet, housing, temperature, and handling. For these pets, ask a vet who treats exotic animals, because general dog and cat advice can be wrong or even harmful for them.

## General Care for Any Pet

A few things apply to almost every pet: fresh, clean water every day, food and feeding made for the species, a clean living space cleaned on a regular schedule, a quick daily check of how the pet looks and behaves, enrichment or play suited to the species, and a vet who knows how to care for that kind of animal.
