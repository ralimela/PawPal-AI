# Building a Pet Care Routine

General guidance only. Adjust any routine to your own pet and your vet's advice.

## Why Routines Matter

Pets do best with consistency. Feeding, walks, play, and medicine at the same times each day help pets feel secure, make house training easier, and make it less likely that you forget something important. A written schedule, like the one in PawPal+, also helps when several people in a household share pet care.

## Sample Daily Routine for a Dog

- Morning: bathroom break and walk, then breakfast.
- Midday: bathroom break, plus a short walk or play if possible.
- Evening: walk, then dinner.
- Before bed: final bathroom break.
- Add a few minutes of training or a puzzle toy most days.

## Sample Daily Routine for a Cat

- Morning: breakfast and fresh water.
- Daytime: a play session of about 10 to 15 minutes.
- Evening: dinner and another play session.
- Every day: scoop the litter box.

## Weekly and Monthly Care Tasks

- Weekly: brushing (more often for long coats), checking ears, washing food and water bowls, and washing pet bedding about every week or two.
- Every few weeks: nail trims.
- Monthly: flea, tick, and heartworm prevention if your vet recommends a monthly product.
- Every 1 to 2 weeks: a full litter box clean for cats.
- Yearly: a wellness checkup, or twice a year for senior pets.

## Medication Reminders

Give medicine at the same time every day, using the schedule your vet gives you. Some medicines should be given with food, so linking them to a meal can help. Set medication tasks at a fixed time so they are easy to remember and so a scheduler can warn you if they clash with something else. Keep a record of each dose you give.

## Caring for Multiple Pets

Give each pet its own schedule and feeding spot, especially if one pet eats faster or eats another pet's food. Stagger tasks that need your full attention, like walks and vet visits, so they don't overlap. Remember that each pet may need different food, medicine, and exercise depending on species, age, and health.
